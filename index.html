08 Quality you can verify

Every report is checked
before it reaches you.

Accuracy is not a claim — it is a process. Here is exactly how we protect every sample that comes through our doors.

Double-verified reporting

Every result is cross-checked by a qualified pathologist before it is released to you or your doctor.

Daily quality controls

Analysers are calibrated and run against control samples every single day before patient testing begins.

Sealed, single-use kits

Fresh sterile needles and sealed vacutainers for every patient, with a documented cold chain from home to lab.

Your data stays private

Reports are shared only with you or a doctor you nominate. We never pass your health data to anyone else.

It depends on the test. Blood sugar (fasting), Lipid Profile and Liver Function Tests usually need 8–12 hours of fasting, while CBC, Thyroid and Vitamin tests do not. When you book, we tell you exactly what is needed.

Yes. Home collection anywhere in Bhubaneswar is free of cost — you only pay for the tests themselves. Slots run from 6 AM to 8 PM, seven days a week.

Most routine tests such as CBC, blood sugar and HbA1c are reported within about 6 hours. Specialised tests like Vitamin D and B12 typically take up to 24 hours. We share reports on WhatsApp and email, and printed copies are available at the centre.
