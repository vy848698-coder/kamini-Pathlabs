Sample to answer

What happens after you hand over a sample.

STEP 01

Collection

A trained phlebotomist draws your sample at home or centre, labelled and sealed the moment it's taken.

STEP 02

Cold-chain transport

Temperature-controlled transport keeps the sample stable, tracked from doorstep to bench.

STEP 03

Automated analysis

Calibrated analysers run your markers — hematology, biochemistry, immunoassay — with minimal human handling.

STEP 04

Two-level check

Instrument QC first, then a qualified pathologist reviews and validates anything that looks off.

STEP 05

Report, explained

Values placed against reference ranges, flags marked clearly, delivered to your phone.
