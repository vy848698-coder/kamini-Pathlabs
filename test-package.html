    01

    Quoted before the needle

    You are told the figure for the whole slip on the phone or at the counter, before anything is drawn. If a test has to be sent out, you are told that too.

    02

    A consultant sometimes adds a test after seeing a result. We call you with the revised figure and wait for your yes before running it.
