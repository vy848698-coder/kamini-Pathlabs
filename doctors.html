04 Your visit

No second trip,
no second waiting room.

At most clinics the consultation ends with a slip and a bus ride to a laboratory. Here the laboratory is through the next door, which is why so many of our patients walk out with the answer rather than an appointment.

  1. 1
    Call and take a slot

    Ring +91 98614 51521 or use the form below. Same-day booking opens at 8.00 AM; we tell you the consultant's fee on the call, before you come.

  2. 2
    See the consultant

    Clinics run to a list, not a queue, so the wait is short. Bring any old reports, prescriptions and the medicines you are taking.

  3. 3
    Give the sample here

    If a test is advised, the draw happens a few steps away — no fresh trip, no fresh appointment, and no bill for a collection charge.

  4. 4
    Report, verified and explained

    Routine results reach WhatsApp in about six hours after a pathologist signs them off. If the consultant is still in clinic, they read it that same evening.

05 Done on site

What your consultant can order
without sending you anywhere.

Every investigation below runs inside the Jagamara centre. Grouped by the consultant most likely to ask for it, so you can see what one visit can settle.

For the ENT clinic

  • Audiometry
  • Tympanometry
  • Laryngoscopy

Hearing thresholds, middle-ear pressure and a look at the voice box — usually finished before the consultation ends.

For the neurology clinic

  • EEG
  • NCV
  • NCS

Brain-wave recording and nerve conduction studies, done here and handed to the same neurologist who ordered them.

For the chest clinic

  • PFT (lung function)
  • Nebulisation

Spirometry to measure how much air you actually move, and a nebuliser on the spot when breathing needs help first.

For the physician

  • ECG
  • Thyroid profile
  • Vitamin profiles
  • HbA1c, LFT, KFT

The routine work behind diabetes, blood pressure and thyroid review — drawn here and reported the same day.

Full pathology

  • All pathological investigations

Haematology, biochemistry, serology and microbiology in our own lab; histopathology and specialised assays through Agilus (SRL), Redcliffe, Thyrocare and GD Labs.

Day procedures

  • Saline insertion
  • Catheterisation
  • Ryle's tube insertion

Small procedures a consultant may want done straight away, handled by our nursing staff without an admission.
