Inside the lab

Behind every result is a process.

Five checkpoints stand between your sample and the number printed on your report.

01
Sample identification

Barcoded at the point of collection, so the tube can't be confused with another.

02
Pre-analytical checks

Volume, clotting and labelling are verified before anything is run.

03
Automated processing

Analysers are calibrated and control samples are run alongside patient samples.

04
Expert validation

Results are reviewed before release, and anything unusual is repeated.

05
Secure reporting

The signed report is released to you — and only to you.
