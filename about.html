01 Our story

We set out to build the lab we would want our own families to walk into.

Kamini Clinic & Labs Jagamara, Khandagiri · Bhubaneswar

It began in a ground-floor room at Alekh Niwas with a microscope, a centrifuge and one collection counter. Jagamara was quieter then. A family that needed a blood count had two options: a long trip into the city, or a wait of several days for a report to travel back.

Neither felt acceptable for something as ordinary as a fever that would not settle. So the brief was simple, and it has not changed since: run the routine work properly, close to where people actually live.

What has changed is everything around that brief. The manual bench gave way to automated analysers. ECG arrived, then nebulisation, then the neurology and lung diagnostics — EEG, NCV, PFT, audiometry — that used to mean a referral across town. Specialists began holding evening clinics upstairs, so a consultation, the test and the report could finally happen at one address on one evening.

Home collection came next, and it stayed free. An 80-year-old with a walking stick should not have to negotiate an auto-rickshaw to give two millilitres of blood. Today our phlebotomists cover most of Bhubaneswar from six in the morning, and the report reaches the family's phone before the day is out.

Eighteen years on we are still a neighbourhood lab, and that is deliberate. It means the person who takes your call is the person you will see at the counter — and that when a value looks wrong, someone here notices, because they remember you.

05 How accuracy is protected

Six things happen before
your report reaches you.

Accuracy is not a claim on a wall. It is a sequence, and every sample goes through all of it.

01

Right patient, right tube

Your name, age and test list are checked against the requisition before a single vial is labelled — the step where most lab errors are actually made.

02

Sealed, single-use kits

A fresh sterile needle and a sealed vacutainer for every patient, opened in front of you, at the centre and at your door alike.

03

A cold chain that holds

Home-collected samples travel in temperature-controlled boxes and are logged in the moment they reach the bench, so nothing sits unaccounted for.

04

Controls before patients

Analysers are calibrated and run against control sera every single morning. If a control drifts, patient testing waits until it is back in range.

05

Anything odd gets repeated

A value that does not fit the clinical picture is re-run on a fresh aliquot rather than reported as-is. It costs us time and saves you a wrong diagnosis.

06

A pathologist releases it

Nothing leaves this lab on an automated print-out alone. A qualified pathologist reviews and signs off every report before it reaches you or your doctor.

Where specialised work is processed

Routine pathology runs in our own lab at Jagamara. Specialised assays, histopathology and cytology are routed to NABL-accredited reference labs we have worked with for years — on the same requisition, with no second trip for you.
