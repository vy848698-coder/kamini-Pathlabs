03 Write to us

Send a note.
We call you back.

Better for anything that needs detail — a prescription with six tests on it, a slot for an elderly parent, a corporate checkup. Here is exactly what happens after you press the button.

  1. 01 Someone reads it at the desk

    Within working hours that is usually a matter of minutes. Nothing is routed to a call centre — it lands with the same team that runs the counter.

  2. 02 We call the number you gave

    At the time of day you picked. We confirm the tests, the exact price, any fasting you need and a collection slot if you want one.

  3. 03 You get it in writing

    The price, the slot and the preparation come to you on WhatsApp, so nothing depends on remembering what was said on a phone call.

What you send stays with Kamini Clinic & Labs. We do not sell or share enquiry details, and we never ask for payment before a test is confirmed. Read the privacy policy.
